## Cave evidence

Digital Dunhuang supplied 28 cave descriptions/periods on 2026-09-28. Northern Liang caves 268/272/275 are referenced through Whitfield, Whitfield and Agnew, Cave Temples of Mogao at Dunhuang (2015), p.55. The 31 chronological examples form a schematic facade, not actual cave positions or a complete inventory.

Examples: cave 420 has flying figures with pipa/harp; 220 a Western Pure Land composition; 320/323/329 lost fragments to Warner in 1924; 112 depicts reverse-held pipa; 17 is the Library Cave; 61 the Mount Wutai image; 3 the eleven-faced, thousand-armed Avalokitesvara. Sources: https://www.e-dunhuang.com/ and https://www.dha.ac.cn/info/1425/3617.htm.

Dunhuang uses GLO-90 N39E094/N40E094; the cliff uses GLO-30 N40E094. The legacy wide grid was cropped from the shared 8-125 / 10-52 degree DEM; flat world assets now handle distant views.

Local extension covers 93-97 E, 38-42 N with 400 by 380 samples. extend_mogao_local_dem.py uses GDAL/numpy and cached GLO-90 context, preserving all original samples. modelBounds/modelBaseMetres preserve the original frame; coreRangeMetres preserves colors. Nonuniform arrays reduce outer density. Local edge alpha is disabled; actual terrain extends the view. Wide/cliff/region data remain unchanged.

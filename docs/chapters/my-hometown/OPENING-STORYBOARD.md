| Scene | Content and behavior |
|---|---|
| 00: Question | Where do you begin to know a land? Photographs, social media, journeys and others' memories appear, followed by an invitation to begin with a book. |
| 01: Book | Two edition covers appear. The chosen book moves centrally, opens and turns pages into full-screen paper. |
| 02: Three lines | Equal-weight passages introduce the Yellow River, Yangtze and rainfall boundary, emerging and fading in sequence. |
| 03: Yellow River | Real geographic context appears; the river is illuminated from source toward the mouth. |
| 04: Cultural roots | Yellow River basin history and comparisons with earlier thinkers accompany the lit river. |
| 05: Yangtze | Southward cultural movement introduces the Yangtze, followed by the five-section reader. |
